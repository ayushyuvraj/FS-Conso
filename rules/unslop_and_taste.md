# Anti-Slop & Unslop Frontend Design Directives (Claude & Taste Guidelines)

This local rulebook sets the non-negotiable aesthetic, typographic, and copy standards for the application.

## 1. Zero AI Slop / Anti-Slop Commandments
- **Banned AI Defaults**:
  - No purple/violet linear gradients or "AI glow" effects.
  - No generic cards-inside-cards with identical gray hairline borders.
  - No floating gradient blobs behind text.
  - No uncalibrated spacing, unpredictable vertical jumps, or random margin inflation.
  - No generic centered hero headlines with three identical equal-width feature boxes.
  - No "placeholder-as-label" in form controls.

## 2. Ruthless Typography & Copy Discipline
- **Every String Must Justify Its Existence**:
  - Before rendering any text, header, badge, or subtitle, ask:
    1. *Is this text strictly required for the user to understand or act?*
    2. *What does this sentence change in how the user decides or acts?*
    3. *If this text is removed, does anything break?*
  - If the answer to any of these is "no", the text is deleted immediately. Zero fluff, zero filler, zero cute micro-meta.
- **Copy Register**:
  - Clean, institutional, crisp, and confident.
  - Financial terms are exact and standardized (`Trial Balance`, `Standardized Import`, `FCTR Adjustment`, `Elimination Entry`, `Consolidated Net`).
  - No patronizing explanations. The tool is an elite financial operations cockpit.

## 3. Visual Restraint & Coherence
- **One Singular Accent Color**:
  - Controlled KPMG palette with high contrast and restrained saturation.
  - Never shift accent colors between sections.
- **Elevation communicates real hierarchy**:
  - Surfaces use subtle tonal contrast (`#FFFFFF` on `#F4F5F7` or dark navy `#001D48`) rather than heavy drop shadows.
- **Responsive Layout Stability**:
  - Viewports remain anchored. No layout jumping or address bar shifts.
